## 2026-08-19 — birth: the tier-2 classification census (01M0D4QV5S follow-on)

Byte-roundtrip is a completeness oracle, not an interpretation oracle —
misclassified trees emit their bytes just fine. This instrument makes
classification health a NUMBER: retained ambiguity (commissio->ambigui,
post-resolution) per file, under the maximum-knowledge house context
(systema_c89 + systema_posix + latina lexica — census wants what the
lexicon CANNOT resolve; examen composes per-file instead, different
mission). Ambigui are attributed to the file whose extent contains them
(propria) vs included content (aliena) so headers count once, at their
own judgment.
