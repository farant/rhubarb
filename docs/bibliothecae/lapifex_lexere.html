lapifex_lexere

Lexer pro Lapifex

The lexer that comes with a lapifex grammar: terminals in the STML grammar carry an 'exemplum', either a literal ('+') or one of two scanners ($numerus for [0-9]+, $identificator for C-style names), and the source is tokenized by those patterns alone. A convenience lexes and parses in one call. Enough for small languages like nuntium's schema language; the C grammar uses arbor2's lexer through the adaptor instead. February 2026.
