sententiae

A reader for 'sentence documents': books of numbered claims where all nesting is written in the address, never in indentation (1.1.1, 4.obj.1.1), as in de-imagine.md (273 sentences in 14 parts). The book is the truth and the tree a projection: the reader never writes, so it can be improved forever without migrating data. Anchors attach to the address the AUTHOR wrote plus a SHA-256 of the normalized text, never to an index the reader invents, and are judged INTEGRA, MOTA (the text moved elsewhere), MUTATA (changed in place) or ORBA (gone); a move is detected but deliberately not followed, because a wrong guess makes an anchor that lies, which is worse than one that breaks. The vocabulary of grades ([principle], [retracted: ...]) is whatever the preface declares, found by position, not phrasing. Its header opens with six traps a naive reader falls into silently, each measured on the real document and pinned by a test (tags spanning continuation lines, prose that looks like an address, the preface ending at the first fence...). Structural faults block with the line named; anomalies are accepted and listed, since they make the projection incomplete, not false. Used by forum.
