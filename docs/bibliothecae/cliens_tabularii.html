cliens_tabularii

Cliens daemonis tabulariumd pro apps

The client that applications use to talk to the ledger daemon (tabulariumd): the MCP dialect over TCP, one connection per request (connect, send one line, read the reply up to a newline, close). If the daemon is absent it starts it once through the launcher, then fails fast rather than freezing the app. Calls take a tool name and JSON arguments, with helpers for reading and for pulling the new record's id out of a reply. Its webview handler exposes a fixed allowlist (addere, gerere, legere, quaerere): that is the whole of what an app may write to the ledger, and the client fills in the actor. Extracted from the forum app so villa could share it; forum and villa_agens are its users.
