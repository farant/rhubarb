thema

The colours. A fixed palette of 16, indexed, with semantic names (background, text, selection, accent, the syntax-colouring roles) mapped onto palette indices, so a view asks for thema_color("what this is") and never names an RGB value. The palette can be swapped as a whole; the default is named Aquinas. Sixteen fixed colours is the same constraint as the dithering in delineare: the house's look is a small fixed palette, the way early terminals and the Mac had it, chosen rather than inherited. The most called function of the stack (thema_color: about 150 calls in 29 files); thema_visus is the view for inspecting and switching themes.
