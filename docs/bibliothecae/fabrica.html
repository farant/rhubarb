fabrica

The house build judge. For each artefact (a file in build/, a committed generated file, an installed binary) it says whether it was made from today's inputs, why not, and in what order to heal it. It never decides by modification time or by mere existence, only by SHA-256 seals of the inputs compared with seals recorded or obtained by regenerating. Actions are declared in aedificatio.stml files (inputs, outputs, command); a kind of artefact says how to observe it, a strategy says how freshness is known (regeneration, the binary's own provenance report, or never judged). It computes dependencies between actions from the places they read and write, orders them, groups those that could run together, heals with fabrica_sanare (judge, run, judge again), and checks that an action wrote only inside its declared footprint. It is pure, touching disk and processes only through a FabricaSutura; bin/fabrica supplies the real one, and commissio refuses a commit whose generated files are stale. Plan 2 (October 2026) adds: actions declared lectiones="verum" are keyed by the verified read log of their last run (lectiones), so the judge sees what an action really read; safe actions heal in parallel (agere_simul, a whole wave checked against the union of footprints) and the judge can foresee regenerations in parallel too; 'families' of actions are written once as an STML template per matching file; and results are cached by content in thesaurus, with bin/compilator caching 'clang -c' per object. Full judgement fell from about 113 s to 96 s and healing nineteen fragments from 93 s to 66 s.
