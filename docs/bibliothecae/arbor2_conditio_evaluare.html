arbor2_conditio_evaluare

Evaluates the constant expressions of #if and #elif: integers, arithmetic, comparison, logical and bitwise operators, the ternary, parentheses, defined(X) and defined X, and macro expansion inside the expression, through arbor2's expansion context. On error it returns 0 and says it failed, rather than guessing. January 2026.
