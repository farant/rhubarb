vicus

hospes applicationum: repositorium unum, tabulae, montationes (insula-rami-plan T1b)

The host of applications: one state repository, one volume, ten tabs, and the applications mounted in them. The name is the Roman street of insulae. A tab is a pair of panes, an editor on the left and on the right a stack whose last member is in front; a pane is one mount, and its id is at once the branch of the repository, the namespace of its document and the scope of its component tree. vicus knows no application: the main registers kinds, each a name, the size of its mount, a function that mounts and one that describes what the host needs from it (registries, Componere, image source, gesture, pulse). It owns the one action registry and the one figure registry the dispatcher holds, and refills them from the focused pane at every switch, so two scriba panes never collide on an action name. A switch is a fixed handover in motus: the leaving pane's gesture is flushed into its own branch (a gesture that will not flush refuses the switch, since text would be lost), pan and zoom likewise, capture is dropped, and only then are the branch and gesture of the arriving pane installed. The first click into an unfocused pane only focuses it, unless the pane asks to act on it. Panes are opened in the stack by commands in text, as requests queued and applied at the next pulse, never inside an event. Living panes such as a terminal are pulsed in the background as well. The layout is configuration in the volume, overwritten and not logged, and a kind this host does not know is kept and not mounted. Begun 2026-10-05 with one application per tab; panes, the stack, Cmd+digit for tabs (Ctrl-A is left to tmux), and a clock and battery reading in the tab bar followed by 10-10.
