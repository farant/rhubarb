iussa in textu, stilo acme (vicus-latera S3a)

Commands in text, in the manner of the acme editor: a word on a page that acts when it is clicked. The form is '$verb' or '$verb(arg, arg)' in a sheet of characters, the verb in lowercase letters, digits, '_' and inner '-', the '$' at the start of a line or after a non-word character. Only verbs the host knows are commands, an idea taken from prunifex, and that removes the need for any escape syntax: '$5.00' and a half-typed '$foo(' stay prose, and an unclosed parenthesis makes the token no command at all, so half a command never runs. The reader is pure: it finds the command that covers a cell, or the next one on a line for colouring. A registry maps verbs to functions that return text to insert or an error to show, and a verb is either consumed by its click (creators such as $dies) or stays in place like a button (openers such as $terminale). The second form is the link, '#word': every such word is a link, either a tag cycled through the pages that carry it, a page by number, or #next, #prev, #first and #last. Written 2026-10-08 for vicus's side panes. scriba_actiones runs the commands and follows the links, scriba_figurae colours them, and vicus registers the verbs.
