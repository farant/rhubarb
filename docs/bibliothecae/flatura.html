flatura

DEFLATE/gzip Compressio et Decompressio

DEFLATE (RFC 1951) and gzip (RFC 1952), both directions, from scratch (the name from flare, to blow: inflate and deflate). Decompress raw DEFLATE or gzip; compress at levels from stored to best, with a 32K window. Named statuses for each way a stream can be bad (data, Huffman tree, distance, allocation, incomplete, gzip header or trailer), and CRC-32 for PNG's use. What makes the house's PNG encoder dependency-free, and what reads git's objects (lib/git.c) and the compressed payloads in capsula and entitas. From December 2025.
