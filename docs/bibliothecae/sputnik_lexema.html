sputnik_lexema

The lexer of sputnik, the house's small scripting language from the first generation (November 2025). Its choices show the language's taste: kebab-case identifiers are allowed, so x-1 is a name and not a subtraction; comments are kept as tokens; template strings were deferred. The first of the three-stage pipeline lexer, parser, interpreter.
