tabula_pixelorum

A pixel buffer and nothing else: width, height, RGBA8888 pixels, RGB/RGBA packing macros, and text drawn and measured in the built-in 6x8 bitmap font. Created bare (tabula_pixelorum_creare_nuda) for tests and offscreen work, or handed out by a window. Split out of fenestra.h on 2026-10-01 (during the tessellatio work, the same day as eventus.h, for the same reason) and also marked <aedilis nexus="purus"/>: rasterizers (tessellatio, pictor_documentum) and terminal programs need pixels but must not link Cocoa. Its implementation still lives in lib/fenestra_textus.c, a name left over from before the split. Everything that draws (delineare, delineare_mandata, imago) draws into one of these.
