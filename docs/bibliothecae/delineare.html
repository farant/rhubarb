delineare

Classic 2D drawing on a TabulaPixelorum, in the spirit of QuickDraw: a context with a clip rectangle and a mode (solid, 8x8 pattern, or XOR), the 38 classic Mac patterns, then lines, rectangles, rounded rectangles, circles, ellipses, triangles, polygons and grids, outlined or filled. Its unusual part is dithering: gradients and colour reduction by Floyd-Steinberg, Atkinson (the original Mac's), or ordered Bayer 4x4/8x8 against a palette, which gives the house its 1-bit, early-Macintosh look. Immediate mode: each call writes pixels now and keeps no scene. The oldest layer of the GUI stack (2025-11) and the most widely used: every *_visus view, the dialogs, elementa, libro_paginarum and arx_caeli draw through it, and delineare_mandata uses it as its backend.
