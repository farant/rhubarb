arbor2_lexema

arbor2's C89 lexer, forked from arbor v1's: newlines become explicit tokens instead of trivia (the preprocessor is line-oriented, so the parser needs to see them), line continuations are tracked, and from phase 2.6 trivia are no longer a blob but explicit tokens of their own (spaces, tabs, continuations, block and line comments) attached before and after each token.
