fractio

Numeri rationales EXACTI

Exact rational numbers over magnus. A Fractio is a numerator and a denominator in canonical form (denominator > 0, gcd 1, zero is 0/1), so equality is equality of parts. Addition and multiplication use Henrici's reduction (Knuth, TAOCP II, 4.5.1): common divisors are removed before multiplying, so intermediates stay small and the result needs no final reduction. Comparison never divides (a*d against c*b). Rounding to an integer is half to even, the IEEE rule, because exact rationals land on exact halves often and the schoolbook rule drifts away from zero over many roundings; floor and ceiling are there to compose the other. Powers take a signed exponent, the rationals being a field. One trap is written into the header: a zero-filled Fractio is 0/0, not zero, and it propagates silently, so values must always be made by fractio_ex_*; Fran chose to document that, not to change the representation. Checked against Python's fractions.Fraction. Born 2026-10-05; situs, laqueus, radices, extensio and polynomium's evaluation stand on it.
