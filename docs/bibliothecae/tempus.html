tempus

horologium et statisticae quadrorum (frame timer)

Time for interactive programs: a global frame clock (delta and total time, a target frame rate with waiting, frame statistics) and independent timers, plus the small mathematics of animation: easing curves (linear, quadratic, cubic, sine, exponential, back, elastic, bounce; each in, out and in-out), approach and spring helpers, interpolation of numbers, angles and colours, clamping (cohibere), smoothstep, waves and remapping. concha and the GUI tests use it. delineare.c has a private cohibere of its own with a different signature, a trap if it ever includes this header.
