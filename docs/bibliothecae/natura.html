natura

The loader for the house's semantic library: taxonomies and dictionaries written as STML files under natura/ (kinds, individual things, and relationships with a name and a converse). The files stay the truth; the loaded graph is a projection. Loading has two phases, like compiling and linking: read each file, then link the whole library, resolving parents across files and enforcing the contract's structural and inheritance rules. Kinds form one Porphyrian chain (genus and species are roles, not natures); individuals have a chain of inheritance and a chain of containment. Queries by name or address, the ancestor chain, and the apparatus a kind inherits (properties, parts, state machines, actions, relations), each with the ancestor that contributed it. It belongs to the knowledge layer rather than the build; bin/natura answers kind lookups for the tools.
