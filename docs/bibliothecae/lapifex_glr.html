lapifex_glr

Motor Parsationis GLR (Generalized LR)

lapifex's GLR engine: consumes the same ACTION/GOTO tables but tolerates conflicts, forking at each one and merging in a graph-structured stack (GSS), with bounds on simultaneous paths and on the number of ambiguous results returned. The generated successor to arbor2's hand-written GLR, built so that C's ambiguities could be described in a grammar rather than coded by hand. Its worklog keeps the lesson of 'phantom forks', invalid reductions from other forks that read the wrong union member and crashed, which is why the C grammar parses a translation unit with a loop instead of a top-level rule.
