laqueus

Laquei polygonales EXACTI in Q^3: nodi et catenae

Exact polygonal knots and links in space, with rational vertices: the first consumer of the whole math chain (laqueus is Latin for a noose). A Laqueus is one or more closed polygons, and every geometric decision goes through situs. laqueus_diagramma projects along a direction and returns the crossings with over, under, sign and exact position, refusing a projection that is not generic; laqueus_diagramma_genericum tries a fixed list of directions and then the curve (1, k, k^2), in a loop bounded by its own counting argument, after an unbounded version turned a planted bug into a ten-minute hang. From a diagram: writhe, linking number, the Kauffman bracket by state sum (refused above 24 crossings, where a different algorithm is owed and not a larger cap), Jones, Alexander by Fox calculus through matrix, and the knot determinant. The same invariants come from a PD code in the KnotTheory and KnotInfo convention, validated down to planarity (a review found the simplifier 'proving' codes of virtual knots to be unknots), and laqueus_pd_simplificare applies Reidemeister moves I and II greedily: no crossing left is a proved unknot. Triangle moves on the polygon are checked operations, and laqueus_simplificare removes vertices by legal moves only. The oracle is the knot table itself: polygons of known knots must give the published Jones and Alexander, and chirality is the physical one (the table's 3_1 is the right-handed trefoil). Born 2026-10-06; tabula_nodorum is generated with it, and knotapel's audit demos from 114 on consume it.
