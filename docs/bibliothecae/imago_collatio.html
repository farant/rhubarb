imago_collatio

Comparing two images and returning a verdict, a count and optionally a picture of the difference, touching no file ('a test that does not touch the environment cannot lie about it'). A pixel differs when its largest channel difference exceeds the tolerance. Anti-aliased edge pixels, which legitimately vary between runs, can be detected and not counted (Kotsarenko and Ramos, the method pixelmatch uses) and are shown yellow. The header records a measurement on real text moved 0.3 of a point: 518 differing pixels, 211 after ignoring anti-aliasing, and a maximum delta of 115, so no tolerance could hide them without hiding real changes; hence a separate allowance for the NUMBER of differing pixels, which grows with edge length, not area. Different sizes are a verdict of their own (the window changed), not an error, and a bounding box names WHERE the change is. Used by specimen for screenshot regression tests.
