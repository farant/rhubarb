internuntius

RPC purum trans suturam missoris (vitrea Phasis A)

Pure RPC over a send-function seam: a JSON envelope ({id, methodus, argumenta} in, {id, fructus} or {id, culpa} out, {eventus, datum} for pushes), a registry of methods, dispatch, and events from C to JS. It knows nothing of WebKit: all output goes through one sender callback, which is 'escape and evaluate JavaScript' in vitrea, an HTTP response in vitrea_servus, and 'append to a captured list' in tests. Replies are routed by id, so slow or out-of-order replies are allowed even though v1 handlers are synchronous. Malformed JSON, unknown methods and handler failures all become error replies and are counted, never crashes. Exposure is a parameter, not a setting: an app registers its methods per transport mode, LOCALIS (its own window, full trust, test harness included) or PUBLICUS (a socket, harness methods excluded), because 'a gate in plain sight is better than a gate you must remember'. Phase A of vitrea (July 2026).
