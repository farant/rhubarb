paginarium

"Codex Paginationis Textuum"

Pagination for plain-text books, especially Project Gutenberg's, whose lines are hard-wrapped at about 70-80 characters: prose must be rewrapped to the widget's width, while verse, lists and tables must keep their lines. The decision is made per paragraph, not per page, by a heuristic: if 80% of a paragraph's first five lines are shorter than 80% of the width, it is verse. Paragraph breaks are two or more newlines. Pages and lines are byte offsets into the source, and a line is rendered on demand (single newlines become spaces in prose). Used by librarium_lector; part of the first-generation reader (Dec 2025).
