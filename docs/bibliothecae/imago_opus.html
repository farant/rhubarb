imago_opus

imagines scalare (image resize, transforms)

Operations on images: scale to exact dimensions or within bounds keeping the aspect ratio, crop then scale, with nearest-neighbour, bilinear, or AREA averaging (alpha-weighted, for shrinking: bilinear reads only a 2x2 neighbourhood whatever the scale, so 1024 to 16 pixels would read 4 of 4096). Since lapide's FR-021 also create a solid image, cut a region without scaling, copy one image into another at an offset (replacing pixels, not blending) and draw a rectangle outline inward, all clipped at the edges rather than refused. Deliberately separate from TabulaPixelorum and delineare, which belong to windows: an Imago stays windowless. Used by tessellatio, icones, tessera's image tools and saltuarius.
