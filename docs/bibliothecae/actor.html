actor

Messages and mailboxes for the idea that 'every entity is an actor': a Nuntius has a sender, a kind ('tick', 'click') and a payload as a hash table of strings, with helpers for text and integer values; a Capsa is an actor's mailbox, created lazily on its first message. The first generation's step toward actors, ten months before the Exemplar programmandi vision (actors, a runtime tree, effects only through servants) restated the idea with a supervision tree. Used by cursus and tractator.
