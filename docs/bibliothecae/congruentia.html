congruentia

Arithmetica modularis EXACTA (Z/n, n < 2^32)

Exact modular arithmetic, Z/n for any modulus 2 <= n < 2^32; the name is Gauss's (Disquisitiones Arithmeticae, art. 1). A residue is a bare i32 with the modulus passed explicitly, no struct, so hot loops pay nothing; products fit in 64 bits, which is why the modulus stops at 2^32 (C89 has no 128-bit type). The contract is deliberately unguarded on the hot path: arguments must already be reduced, and the header says what happens otherwise. Composite moduli are allowed, the inverse refusing when gcd(a, n) is not 1. Primality is Miller-Rabin with bases 2, 7 and 61, deterministic for every 32-bit number; the review ran it against a sieve of every n below 2^32 with no mismatch. congruentia_primus_infra walks down the primes to supply moduli for the Chinese remainder theorem, and congruentia_restitue rebuilds a Magnus from residues by Garner's method, as the symmetric representative if asked. It backs the Z/n ring in anulus; multimodular determinants and normal forms are the consumer planned next (docs agenda, A8). Born 2026-10-06.
