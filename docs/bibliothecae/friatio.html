functiones friationis (hash functions)

Hash functions in one place: fast non-cryptographic hashes for tables (FNV-1a, DJB2), cryptographic digests (SHA-1, kept for UUIDv5 and old checksums and marked obsolete for security, and SHA-256), and CRC32 for integrity checks. Each has a one-shot form and an incremental one (initiare, addere, finire), over chordae or raw bytes. tabula_dispersa and internamentum hash with it.
