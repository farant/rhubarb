tempestivum

Timers as entities: a timer is an entity of genus 'System::Tempestivum' with properties for its target, the message kind to send, the interval, whether it repeats, the next firing time and whether it is active. An administrator checks them once per frame and sends due messages through the actor bus (cursus). Because timers are entities, they persist and can be inspected like anything else. The ludus design handles time the other way round: as a field of each event, with a pulse when nothing arrives.
