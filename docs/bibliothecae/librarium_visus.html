librarium_visus

A library browser for the books listed in librarium.stml: a main menu (all, by title, by tag), a list of tags, a catalogue in compact or full form, one book's metadata, and reading, all paginated rather than scrolled, navigated with j/k, h/l, Enter and Escape. The reading itself is done by librarium_lector. The visible end of the Gutenberg work of December 2025.
