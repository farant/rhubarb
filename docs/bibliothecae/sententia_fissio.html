sententia_fissio

textum in sententias findere (sentence split)

Splits prose into sentences, the hard way: abbreviations (Mr., Dr.), straight and curly quotes, decimals ($3.50), ellipses, dialogue attribution ('"Hello!" she said.' is one sentence), and Roman numerals after names (George II.). Three forms of the same result: copies, byte offsets, or a lazy iterator that can step back as well as forward and caches what it has split. Written for the Gutenberg reader (Dec 2025); sententia_paginarium is its consumer.
