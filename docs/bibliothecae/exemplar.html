exemplar

A small regular-expression engine: any character, *, + and ?, character classes with ranges and negation, ^ and $ anchors, and escapes, compiled once and matched to give the position and length of the first match. Thompson's NFA construction, so matching time is guaranteed O(n*m) and a pathological pattern cannot blow up as it can in backtracking engines. Deliberately a subset; the name means 'pattern'. From December 2025; today only its tests call it.
