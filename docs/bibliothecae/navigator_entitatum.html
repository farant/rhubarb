navigator_entitatum

Browses the entity graph from the keyboard in two columns, the parent dimmed on the left (30%) and the current entity with a selection on the right (70%): j and k move, l follows a relation or opens a property, h goes back along the path. Navigation can follow back-links, and the path is kept as history. Starts at the repository's first root. One of the first widgets (2025-11-20), and the most direct view of the first generation's data model.
