numerus_romanus

numeri Romani legere, scribere (numerals)

Roman numerals, read and written. Reading accepts only the strict subtractive form, because a loose rule ('all letters in IVXLCDM') would accept DID, MILD, CIVIC, VIM, DIM, LIVID and MIMIC as numbers, and each of those can stand in a chapter index; MIX survives, as it must, being a real number. Lowercase is accepted (preface pages are i-xlii), mixed case is not (Xii is a word). Writing gives the canonical form up to MMMCMXCIX. numerus_romanus_exprimere goes further: the C89 expression the house writes for any number, in latina.h's vocabulary (ZEPHYRUM, CVI, IV * M for 4000, IV * MXXIV for 4096, keeping binary intent), adding an (i64) cast where the product would overflow int. It is written for the formatter (a filed wish) and used today by fabrica; latina.h's numeral block comes from numerus_romanus_scribere. Born in capitula (chapter headings) and promoted when a second consumer (page numbers) arrived.
