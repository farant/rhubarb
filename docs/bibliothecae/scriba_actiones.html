scriba_actiones

tractator paginae scribae (scriba-plan S1c)

scriba's one action handler, pagina.clavis: keys and committed text become vim on a working sheet, the view's private copy of the page, held as a gesture in motus. vim's state between keys (cursor, mode, selection, previous key, clipboard) lives in the island, and the document is written only when the gesture is flushed. The saving rule is Fran's: in normal and visual mode a change is complete when its key is and is committed at once; in insert mode the sheet is committed after about a second without a key, and Esc commits and closes the insert. The first chunk of an insert is a plain act and later chunks are joined to it, so one u takes back the whole insert; u and Ctrl-R go to the document's history and not to vim's own undo. Printable characters are taken only from committed text, because real sources send a key-down as well and each letter would otherwise arrive twice. Written 2026-10-05. Since 10-08 a view may share its page with other views (its sheet is re-copied when the history has moved without it), a click places the cursor and starts a new undo unit, Ctrl+Shift+arrows move between the pages of a scriba_liber, and words in the text can act: a known $command under a click runs as one undoable change, and a #tag is a link to the next other page that carries it, Shift going backwards. Limits: bytes above 0x7F are ignored, and the house vim is not vim (no yank, line-wise visual only).
