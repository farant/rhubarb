sententia_paginarium

"Paginatio Sententiarum"

Pages a text by sentence rather than by screen: in prose the unit is a sentence, in verse a line (because of enjambment), and a paragraph break is an empty unit; each unit is word-wrapped to the given width when it is first asked for, and cached until the size changes. Built on paginarium's verse/prose detection and sententia_fissio. The reading mode of the Gutenberg reader (librarium_lector): one sentence at a time, the opposite of a page of 25 lines.
