calendarium_liturgicum

The Roman liturgical calendar (the General Roman Calendar of 1969, ordinary form). For any date it gives the liturgical season and week, the celebrations of the day with their rank and colour, the Sunday (A, B, C) and weekday (I, II) lectionary cycles and the psalter week; for a year, the movable feasts, all computed from Easter through fasti's computus. The fixed saints' days are a table in a second file, sanctorale.c, which the header names with an aedilis annotation; their names are English, with the US holy days of obligation marked. The calendar view (calendario_visus) is its user.
