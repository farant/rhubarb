calendario_visus

The liturgical calendar as a month view: the selected day's details in the top third (date, feast, liturgical season and cycle, from calendarium_liturgicum) and the month's grid below, each day number in its liturgical colour, with vim keys for day (h/l), week (j/k), month (H/L) and year (J/K). Opens on today. A panel mode of schirmata (December 2025).
