js_lexema

A total lexer for JavaScript under silva's contract (total, byte-faithful, byte positions, trivia as tokens, unknown bytes as an IGNOTUM kind). Its scope is the house dialect, ES5 plus what the house writes (class, arrow functions, template literals, let and const), though later syntax still lexes, since tokens barely change between versions and growth belongs to the tree. Automatic semicolon insertion is the tree's concern: the lexer keeps newlines verbatim in its whitespace runs. Only its tests use it today; the decree that fixed the order of materia's clients names JavaScript next. August 2026.
