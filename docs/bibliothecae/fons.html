fons

Maps Unicode codepoints to the 256 glyphs of the 6x8 bitmap font: ASCII and Latin-1 directly, curly quotes to straight ones, en and em dashes to a hyphen, the ellipsis to a full stop, a few symbols to their places in the font, and everything else to a tofu glyph. Used when the window draws text (fenestra_textus). December 2025.
