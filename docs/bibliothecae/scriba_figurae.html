figurae scribae (scriba-plan S2)

scriba's figures: how the desk, the sheet and the status line are drawn, pure like every figure. The sheet is paper on a desk inside a frame, with its text, the selection as whole lines, and a steady block cursor (no blinking: there is one clock, and blinking from wall time would be forbidden) under which the character is redrawn in the paper colour. The text comes through the figure's context, the working sheet, so an insert in progress is visible before it is saved. Known $commands are redrawn in one accent colour and, when there is a page book, #tags in the other. The status line shows the mode word in its colour and the position, and to the right the page indicator and a message in the error colour. Two findings from 2026-10-05. A planted fault that ignored the mode in the cursor's colour survived, because in the theme the two colours were the same gold; the cursor now takes its mode's status colour and the test asserts that they differ. And Fran saw that in the terminal the frame could be typed over: the cell rasteriser rounds a rectangle to the nearest cell, so a frame one pixel outside the paper landed on the paper's own cells. The frame now sits a whole cell out, in the margin, and pictor received the same fix.
