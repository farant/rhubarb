capitula

Reads a book's table of contents written once by the note-taker, the way it is printed in the book itself ('I - De methodo', '1. Quid quaeratur', 'Appendix A - Tabulae', with indentation for depth), and turns each line into a chapter that becomes a real entity in the store, to which notes, tags and links can attach. A pure function: text in, structure out. It never refuses, and the header explains why that differs from sententiae: a table of contents has no invariant that a line can break (any non-empty line is a chapter), while two sentences claiming one address would make a false projection. Roman chapter numbers are read through numerus_romanus, where it was born. Used by forum (August 2026).
