stilus_terminalis

Stilus cellulae et SGR (modulus 004; planum

The style of a terminal cell and its SGR codec in both directions. One model: three colours (text, background, underline), each native, a palette index or RGB; eight attribute flags; six kinds of underline. stilus_codificare writes the canonical full reset (ESC [ 0, the attributes in a fixed order, then the colours), with RGB either in full or quantised to the 256 palette; stilus_applicare is the decoder, Ghostty's SGR parser (sgr.zig at a pinned commit) translated, which accepts ';' and ':' separators in every colour form, counts unknown parameters and never fails, and leaves alone a CSI that only looks like SGR (the xterm sequence ESC [ > 4 ; 2 m must not read as underline plus faint). It also holds xterm's default 256 colours and the nearest-colour quantiser that tessera used to own. Pure, with no allocation beyond the caller's string builder. Built 2026-10-03 as module 004 of the terminal plan. The round trip over 9,216 generated styles found a real bug: a full style is 26 parameters, terminals cap a CSI at 24 and drop the whole sequence, and nobody had ever sent one; the encoder now writes parameters in groups that are never split and opens a second sequence when needed. A planted fault also showed that tessera's byte goldens had never pinned any attribute, only colours. tessera emits through it (its amalgam vendors a copy), aemulator decodes with it, and terminale reads the result.
