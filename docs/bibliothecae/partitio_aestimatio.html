partitio_aestimatio

Held-out scoring of a partition for oratio (decisions 49-53 of its spec): three partitions of the same rows, the candidate, the gold answers and the lots (corpora). For each lot held out, the other lots vote: a group carries a vote when it has enough training rows, the gold class with the most training rows wins strictly, and ties fall back to a base answer; rows in groups without a vote are answered by the base and counted as unscored. Integers only, per mille, never floats. It also classifies each group's behaviour (forced, ordered, open). September 2026.
