codificator_terminalis

Eventus -> octeti terminalis (eventus B6a;

The inverse of the decoder: given an Eventus and the modes the program asked for, the bytes a terminal would send it (legacy with xterm modifiers, SS3, modifyOtherKeys and fixterms forms; kitty with flags, alternate keys, associated text and event kinds; SGR mouse and paste). The oracle is Ghostty's key encoder (pinned commit), its canonical forms followed exactly; what a mode cannot express is consumed and writes nothing. Pure: modes are an argument, since the emulator will own them. With it, a recorded session can be replayed through a terminal: encode, decode, dispatch (manus_ludus_iterare_per). Eventus B6a, October 2026.
