paginatio

Page designations in a real book, which carries TWO series of pages: roman numerals for the front matter (i to xlii) and arabic for the body (1 to 380), so 'xii' and '12' are different pages and an integer alone cannot say which. Accepts strict roman numerals (upper or lower case) and positive integers; rejects zero, and rejects '12a', 'A-3' or 'p. 12', because looseness would put a page in the wrong order and tie a note to the wrong place. A TYPE, judged by the ledger's genus machinery (gesta), so a malformed designation cannot enter the store by any route. Named paginatio because pagina was already the editor's (August 2026).
