sectio

Not text: sections of Mach-O binaries (macOS executables and object files). Iterate every section of every segment (LC_SEGMENT_64 and LC_SEGMENT) or find one by segment and section name (__TEXT, __text), then read its name, data (zero-copy, pointing into the loaded binary), size, address, alignment and flags, with the section type and attribute constants from mach-o/loader.h. Built on macho.h, from the early binary-inspection work (Nov 2025); today only its tests use it, alongside macho's users (symbola).
