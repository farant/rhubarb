schirmata

Ten screens switched like tmux (ctrl-a then n, p or a digit), with a tab bar at the bottom: each screen has its own widgets and remembers its open page, cursor and vim mode, while the book (libro) and the repository are shared. The right-hand panel switches between modes, each a singleton viewer: the card castle (arx_caeli), themes, fonts, the syntax reference, the Bible, the library, the calendar, the gallery and image import, plus a modal dialog. In effect the first generation's whole application (the Combinado demo) in one controller. Used by concha.
