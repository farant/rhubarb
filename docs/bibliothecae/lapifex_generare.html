lapifex_generare

Generator Grammaticae GLR

lapifex's parser generator: reads a grammar written in STML, computes FIRST and FOLLOW sets, and builds the parse table. The canonical LR(1) collection exploded on real grammars (the 95-production C expression grammar ran without end, since every distinct lookahead makes a new state), so on 2026-02-09 an LALR(1) builder was added: an LR(0) automaton first, then lookaheads propagated to a fixed point, about 155 states and two seconds instead of tens of thousands. Its header opens with a boxed warning that is the best summary of the generation: 'LIVE CONSUMER: lib/nuntium_schema.c'. lapifex is silva's ancestor (arbor v1, arbor2, lapifex, silva) and retired as a C parser, but nuntium, which is alive, compiles its schemas with this engine: two roles, of which the file tree shows only one, so these files cannot be archived or deleted while nuntium needs them, even though the tests of the retired role could be moved (and were, to archivum/, on 2026-09-02).
