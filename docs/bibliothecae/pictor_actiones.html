pictor_actiones

tractatores pictoris (P3: penicillus.ictus,

pictor's action handlers, registered by name into an actio registry: penicillus.ictus (a brush stroke, through its phases from the event kind) and instrumentum.eligere (choose a tool). They perform no I/O; they write only through gates: the islands through mutare_*, motion through mutare_motum, and the document through pictor_documentum_actum, its own gate. One action per intention, as the ludus rules ask. Small on purpose: two handlers are enough to carry the whole state doctrine through a real app.
