pictor_actiones

tractatores pictoris (P3: penicillus.ictus, instrumentum.eligere)

pictor's action handlers, registered by name into an actio registry, one per intention, the phase coming from the event's kind. Four of them draw. The brush, the spray can, the sponge and the line each capture the pointer, collect points in motus, and on release write ONE stroke act into the document, so a stroke is one undo step and never touches the island. Which of the four the canvas runs is decided when the tree is composed, from the current tool, so no handler ever branches on the tool. The spray records a seed and the time of each point, so that its randomness replays, and its release point as well, since holding still before letting go adds dots. The line is click, click with a rubber-band preview: it keeps the capture between the two clicks so that hover motion reaches it, Shift chains segments, and Esc or a click outside the canvas cancels. The others set state: opening a palette from a square of the tool bar, choosing a colour, a pattern, a size (sizes are per tool) or a tool, by click or by key (p, a, e, l), and the layers panel, where one handler selects, hides, adds and deletes layers and drags them into a new order. Cmd+Z and Cmd+Shift+Z undo and redo; Cmd+V pastes an image as a new layer. They perform no I/O: the island is written through its gate, the document through pictor_documentum_actum, its own gate, and the clipboard is a port the application installs. Closing a palette is no longer theirs since dimissio took it over. One bug worth keeping (2026-10-08): during a capture the stroke read the point local to whatever lay under the pointer, another pane or the status line, and the line jumped to the far edge; the point is now converted into the captured canvas's own space. It began in September as two handlers, enough to carry the whole state doctrine through a real app, and grew in October to ten handlers under eleven names as pictor became usable.
