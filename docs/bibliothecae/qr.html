qr

QR Code Generatio

QR code generation (ISO/IEC 18004), versions 1 to 10, byte mode, four error-correction levels, returning a grid of modules. Data too large for version 10 is refused by name, never encoded wrongly. Its verification is the story: on 2026-08-01, 66 structural tests passed while every version from 4 up was unreadable, because block interleaving was missing; since then a round-trip script has macOS's CoreImage, a decoder the house did not write, read back what was generated. Used by forum (pairing a phone by scanning).
