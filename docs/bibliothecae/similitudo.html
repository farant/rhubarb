similitudo

congruentia nebulosa, genus fzf (fuzzy matching)

Fuzzy matching like fzf, for identifiers: the 'net to throw' when the exact name is not known. A query matches if it is a case-insensitive subsequence of the candidate; the score rewards word boundaries (start, after _ - . / : or space, a lowercase-to-uppercase change), continuous runs and exact case, and penalizes gaps. similitudo_optima_decurtata also tries the query's prefixes (at least three characters), because a name that is right at the start and wrong at the end matches nothing as a whole, which is what a person in fzf fixes by deleting; a shorter clean prefix can beat a longer dirty one. Pure: no arena, no allocation, no state. Greedy left-to-right; an optimal (dynamic programming) alignment is a named deferral under the same API. Born 2026-07-10; used for 'did you mean' in canon, legatus, silva's emitte and nexus, and briar.
