latina

Verba clavis C89 Latine reddita (lexicon domus)

The house's dialect: C89 keywords in Latin (si/alioquin, per, dum, redde, structura, nomen = typedef, magnitudo = sizeof...), the fixed-width types (i8..i64 UNSIGNED, s8..s64 signed, f32/f64, b32, memoriae_index), NIHIL/VERUM/FALSUM, and every Roman numeral from I to MMMCMXCIX as a macro, so code writes CCLVI instead of 256 (ZEPHYRUM is 0). The numeral block is generated by numerus_romanus_scribere and judged by the 'generata' gate; larger values are written as expressions (IV * M, IV * MXXIV). Two consequences every newcomer meets: each of these words is a reserved identifier (never name a variable nomen, per, duplex or MD), and system headers that use such names must be included BEFORE latina.h. A trap for readers who know Rust, Zig or Odin, where i32 is the SIGNED type: here i32/i64 are unsigned (read i as insignatus, s as signatus, which is our reading and not stated in the header); the s/b names follow Handmade Hero's s32/b32, and -Wsign-conversion catches most slips. Included by everything; one of the house's first files (2025-10-21).
