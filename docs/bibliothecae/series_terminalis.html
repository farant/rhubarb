series_terminalis

Lexemator fluminis terminalis (DEC/Williams)

The lexer for the byte stream of a terminal, in both directions (what a program writes for an emulator, and what the terminal sends back: keys, mouse, paste, replies). Bytes in, tokens out (print, execute, ESC, CSI, SS, OSC, DCS, APC, abandoned sequences), and no meaning: what CSI 'A' means is for the layers above. No time, no UTF-8, no allocation after creation. The state machine is Paul Williams's DEC parser as Ghostty implements it (pinned commit, MIT), with each deliberate divergence listed: C1 bytes are not recognized (they are UTF-8 continuations), kitty's ':' separators are kept, an interrupted sequence becomes a named FUGA token with its raw bytes rather than vanishing, and a sequence with too many parameters is dropped whole ('half an SGR is worse than none'). Eventus B1a, October 2026.
