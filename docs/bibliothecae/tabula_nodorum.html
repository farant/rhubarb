tabula_nodorum

Tabula nodorum primorum (usque ad XIII transitus, XIIDCCCCLXVI nodi) et agnitio per polynomia Alexander et Jones

The table of prime knots up to 13 crossings, 12,966 of them, and recognition of a knot from its Alexander and Jones polynomials. Names, crossing numbers, symmetry types and PD codes come from KnotInfo (Livingston and Moore), through a pinned snapshot of database_knotinfo dated 2026.10.5; the polynomials are not copied but computed by laqueus from the PD codes, then collated against KnotInfo's own columns: 12,966 of 12,966 equal, chirality included, which makes the table the strongest outside oracle laqueus has. A name means the table's diagram and 'K*' its mirror; the table's 3_1 is the right-handed trefoil. tabula_nodorum_agnoscere returns every candidate whose two polynomials match, primes and sums of two table knots, in either chirality, and the header is plain that this is a match and not a proof of type: 5_1 and 10_132* share both polynomials, the Conway and Kinoshita-Terasaka knots both have Alexander 1, a review counted 95 primes that look like a sum (the smallest, 9_12, like 4_1 # 5_2), and a chiral knot with a symmetric Jones comes back twice. The data file is generated (lib/tabula_nodorum_data.c, compared byte for byte by the generata gate), and the house around it changed its shape twice: PD codes became strings when 657,484 integers passed silva's cap of 2^20 tokens per file, and every string moved into a named array when macOS was found copying each test binary's string literals into its log cache, 42 GB in a day. October 2026.
