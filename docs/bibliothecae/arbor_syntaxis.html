arbor_syntaxis

arbor v1's parser: a Pratt parser for expressions and recursive descent for statements, building a uniform node (53 kinds, a union for kind-specific data, children in a Xar) that keeps trivia for round trips, records origins from the preprocessor, and recovers from errors with ERROR nodes. The design that was outgrown: real C cannot be parsed in one pass without knowing which names are types and which are macros, and those definitions often come through includes the parser cannot see. A single-path recursive descent has to guess; arbor2 moved to GLR, which can follow several readings at once and keep 'probably a typedef' or 'probably a macro' nodes (the arbor-v2 brainstorm records the reasoning).
