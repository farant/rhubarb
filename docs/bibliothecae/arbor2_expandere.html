arbor2_expandere

arbor2's macro expansion engine: expansion in LAYERS (layer 0 the source tokens, layer N after N expansion passes) over SEGMENTS (ranges where the set of macros does not change, cut at #define and #undef), with full provenance. It also records the typedefs it discovers, because the GLR parser needs to know which names are probably types. The design answers the arbor-v2 brainstorm's question of how to parse C whose macros and typedefs may come from files the parser cannot see. January 2026.
