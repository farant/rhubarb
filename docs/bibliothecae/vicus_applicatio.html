vicus_applicatio

compositio communis hospitis (insula-rami-plan T4)

vicus assembled: what its window main and its terminal twin share. It is here, and not in vicus, that the applications are known. The kinds scriba, pictor and terminale are registered with their mount and describe functions; the default layout is written (ten tabs: scriba beside a terminal, scriba beside pictor, then scriba beside scriba); and the dispatcher is bound over the host's repository. It also owns what panes of one kind share: one page book and one command registry for all scriba views, and one cache of drawings and one bundle of state systems for all pictor panes. The commands that pages can run are registered here: $dies inserts the date; $terminale, $scriba and $pictor open a pane in the right-hand stack; $pictor-next, $pictor-prev and $pictor-new change the drawing shown. Shared pages are kept honest at two points, in the pulse and when focus arrives, the second because a click into a stale view would otherwise type onto the old sheet and revert the other view's text. Written 2026-10-06 (insula-rami plan T4) with a replay proof across both targets: a window session replayed through the terminal encoder, which found that the test hand's mouse presses carried no button and were being dropped.
