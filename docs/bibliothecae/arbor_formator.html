arbor_formator

arbor v1's formatter, with two modes: faithful (an exact byte-for-byte round trip, trivia preserved) and formatted (the house style, taken from lib/piscina.c: Allman braces, types right-aligned with a two-character gutter, assignments aligned on '=', return types on their own line and one parameter per line, spaced casts and returns). The house style it encoded survives in silva's formatter; this implementation is the first attempt. January 2026.
