## Description

Identify, understand, and ultimately build "wisdom circuits" — the abstract relational patterns that enable generalization across domains. These are domain-independent circuits that multiple domain-specific inputs route through:

- **Asymmetric dependency**: parent->child, cause->effect, premise->conclusion
- **Part-whole composition**: element->set, member->group, chapter->book
- **Cyclic return**: seasonal patterns, liturgical cycles, recursive structures
- **Hierarchy of participation**: Thomistic participation, inheritance, prototype chains

In Thomistic terms, these correspond to sapientia — not knowledge of particulars (scientia), but understanding of the structural principles that organize all other knowledge.

Wisdom circuits can live in all three architectural layers:
- **Syllogism layer**: tensor logic equations themselves ARE wisdom circuits (Barbara is domain-independent)
- **Fluency layer**: learned representations of linguistic structure (ablative absolute = simultaneous background condition)
- **Interface**: mappings between linguistic form and logical content ("Omnis X est Y" = universal affirmative)
- **Spanning both**: analogy requires BOTH fluency and logic (parse four terms + encode proportionality)

## Why It Matters

The 03-transparency document argues that wisdom circuits are the mechanism behind LLM generalization: "There are algebraic patterns of relationships between objects in reality, and once you have internal circuits that match those patterns, inputs from different domains start sharing those circuits. That IS generalization."

Understanding this mechanism is what separates eigenflower from both black-box LLMs (which have wisdom circuits but cannot inspect them) and pure logic engines (which have transparent circuits but only for deduction). The goal is transparent wisdom circuits — understood, compiled, and composable.

DKC results directly inform this:
- Depth law: recursive application builds computational power (wisdom circuits gain power through recursion)
- Algebraic coherence beats vocabulary: shared structure matters more than raw diversity
- Parity-lock theorem: how you present information determines what can be computed (the routing problem)

## What "Achieved" Looks Like

- Taxonomy of abstract relational patterns relevant to theological reasoning
- At least 3-5 wisdom circuits identified with precise mathematical structure
- At least one wisdom circuit demonstrated to generalize across domains (same circuit, different bindings)
- Architecture for routing inputs through the correct wisdom circuit
- Clear connection to DKC theoretical framework
- Documented relationship to Thomistic hierarchy of knowledge (scientia, intellectus, sapientia)
