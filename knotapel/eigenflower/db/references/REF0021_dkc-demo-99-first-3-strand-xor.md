## Description

Demo 99 (LANDMARK) achieved the first-ever 3-strand XOR computation in DKC, using the irreducible Delta_1 module over Z[zeta_8]. This proved that multi-strand DKC works and dramatically extends capacity beyond the 2-strand regime. XOR14=60 solutions were found, with a ceiling at XOR16=0.

## Relevance to Eigenflower

- Demonstrates that DKC's algebraic structure scales to richer braid groups, not just 2-strand
- Multi-strand = more computational resources available for the reservoir, directly relevant to reservoir capacity in the DKC-inspired reasoning layer
- The strand count is a new scaling axis orthogonal to depth and activation resolution
