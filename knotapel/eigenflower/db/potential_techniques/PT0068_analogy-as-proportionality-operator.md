Model analogical reasoning as structural mapping via proportionality operators. "Grace is to the soul as light is to the eye" -- the fluency layer parses the four terms, the syllogism/reasoning layer encodes the proportionality relation A:B::C:D.

In the Hilbert space framework, word analogy "king - man + woman = queen" is a spectral shift -- modulation of frequency components. The proportionality operator preserves relational structure while mapping between domains.

For eigenflower: analogical reasoning requires BOTH layers (parsing four terms = fluency, encoding proportionality = reasoning). Wisdom circuits spanning both layers are the most interesting case.

Aquinas heavily uses analogy (especially analogia entis -- the analogy of being between God and creatures). A transparent analogical reasoning component would handle a significant fraction of the Summa's arguments that are NOT strictly syllogistic.

Mathematical structure: morphisms between relations. Category theory provides the formal framework. Whether this can be compiled as transparently as syllogisms is an open research question.
