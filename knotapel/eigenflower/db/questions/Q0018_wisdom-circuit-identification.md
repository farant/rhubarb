Wisdom circuits are hypothesized domain-independent circuits that handle abstract relational patterns (asymmetric dependency, part-whole composition, cyclic return, hierarchy of participation). If the circuit sharing thesis is correct, these should be identifiable as recurring algebraic patterns in weight matrices — the same structure appearing in attention heads that process family trees, causal chains, and logical inference. The experiment: take a model known to handle multiple relational reasoning tasks, decompose its weights via GPTQ-as-CVP on cyclotomic lattices, and look for shared algebraic motifs across weights that serve different domains. Finding such motifs would be strong evidence for the circuit sharing thesis and would give a blueprint for building wisdom circuits deliberately.
