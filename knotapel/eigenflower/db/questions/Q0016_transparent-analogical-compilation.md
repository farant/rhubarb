---
id: Q0016
title: "Can analogical reasoning (A:B::C:D proportionality) be compiled transparently like syllogistic reasoning?"
status: open
source: "notes/03-transparency-and-wisdom-circuits.md"
related: []
---

Syllogistic deduction has known mathematical structure (tensor equations) and can be compiled transparently. Analogical reasoning — "A is to B as C is to D" — also has mathematical structure (morphisms between relations, proportionality). The question is whether this structure is concrete enough to compile into exact algebraic operators with DKC-level transparency: knowing what every weight does, what the activation does, and why certain combinations compute certain analogical inferences. If yes, the "wisdom circuits" program gains a second transparent reasoning mode beyond deduction. If no, analogy remains in the "fluency layer handles it statistically" territory, which is a significant limitation since Aquinas uses analogy extensively.
