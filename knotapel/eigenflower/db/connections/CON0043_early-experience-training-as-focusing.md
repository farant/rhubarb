Paper 2 distinguishes imitation learning (expert demos), reinforcement learning (reward signals), and early experience (consequences alone). DKC transcends all three: bracket values are exact, computed from topology, not approximated from data. Training does not CREATE computational structure; it LOCATES pre-existing solutions on the Z[zeta_8] lattice.

Framed as constructive proof that early experience works:
1. "Expert trajectory" = braid word producing correct XOR triple
2. "Alternative actions" = other braid words at same depth producing different values
3. "Next-state prediction" = predicting bracket value from braid word (IWM objective)
4. "Self-reflection" = understanding WHY a triple computes XOR (Dual-Channel Theorem)

Paper 2's finding that STaR-style rationales DEGRADE when ungrounded maps to D85-86: post-hoc rationalization of indecomposability parameter b = -5/8 diverged at larger lattice sizes. Only grounded computation (direct delta-parameterized Gram matrix at TL_4) gave correct results.

The "environment" is algebraic structure of the cyclotomic ring, "actions" are bracket evaluations, and "consequences" are truth tables. No reward needed because algebraic structure IS the information.
