title: "Whether the circuit sharing thesis (generalization = shared abstract circuits) is empirically verifiable"

related: []

The thesis is: LLMs generalize because there are algebraic patterns of relationships in reality, and once a model develops internal circuits matching those patterns, different domains share those circuits. This is presented as an explanation for emergence. But it is unclear whether this thesis is empirically testable in a way that distinguishes it from alternative explanations for generalization (memorization + interpolation, feature learning, in-context learning, etc.). The GPTQ-as-CVP decomposition approach is proposed as a test, but even if it finds shared algebraic motifs, establishing that these motifs CAUSE generalization (rather than being correlated artifacts) is a harder claim.
