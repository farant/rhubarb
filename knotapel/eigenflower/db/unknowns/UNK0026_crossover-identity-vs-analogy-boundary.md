The analysis carefully distinguishes connections that are "mathematical identities" (Group D crossover) from those that are "conceptual parallels" or "analogies" (parts of Group A, most of Group E). But the boundary between these categories is itself unclear in some cases. For example, "DKC IS tensor logic over Boolean tensors" is called a mathematical identity, but DKC operates over Z[zeta_8] with specific activation functions, while Domingos's tensor logic is formulated over general Boolean tensors with Heaviside — the two share structure but differ in specifics. How much discrepancy is allowed before an "identity" becomes merely an "analogy"? The strength of the entire eigenflower-knotapel bridge depends on getting this classification right.
