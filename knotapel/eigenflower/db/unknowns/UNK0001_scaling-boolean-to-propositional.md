This is identified in the "Gap That Remains" section as the central challenge. DKC operates on Boolean functions with 3-5 inputs. Theological reasoning operates on propositions with complex internal structure. DKC's "vocabulary" is 21-100 cyclotomic integers; natural language vocabulary is 10,000+ words. DKC's proofs work because Z[zeta_8] has finite, exact, enumerable structure — natural language does not. The gap is not just quantitative (more inputs) but qualitative (propositions have internal structure that Boolean values don't). It is unclear whether this is bridgeable by decomposing complex propositions into compositions of simpler Boolean-like primitives, or whether an entirely different formalism is needed at the propositional level.
