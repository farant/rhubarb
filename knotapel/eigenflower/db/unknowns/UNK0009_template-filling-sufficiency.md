title: "Whether template-constrained generation is sufficient for narrating logical derivations"
status: open

related: []
---

The architecture assumes narration is "closer to template filling than free generation" because the logical structure constrains what needs to be said. This is an untested assumption. Simple syllogisms ("All M are P, all S are M, therefore all S are P") may indeed be templatable. But the Summa's actual arguments often involve chains of multiple syllogisms, distinctions between senses of terms, appeals to authority, and responses to objections — all of which require flexible natural language expression that may exceed what templates can provide. The gap between template-filling and full generation is large, and the right level of constraint is unknown.
