The analysis notes that "agents sometimes rated relevance generously" — for example, Orchid's "MEDIUM" for syllogism compilation is described as "really a stretch." This raises a broader concern: across all 14 papers and 5 groups, how many relevance ratings are overstatements? The crossover analysis partially addresses this by distinguishing mathematical identities from analogies, but the paper extraction ratings themselves may have inflated how relevant certain techniques are. This matters because build priorities are partly informed by these ratings. We don't have a systematic way to audit the ratings other than manual review.
