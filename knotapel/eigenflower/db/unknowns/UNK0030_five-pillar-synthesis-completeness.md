The five-pillar synthesis (topology, algebra, neural computation, information theory, reservoir computing) emerged from knotapel's broad scope. DKC's theoretical framework rests on all five. But is five complete? The crossover analysis mentions connections to areas not obviously covered by the five pillars: tensor decomposition (multilinear algebra, partially covered by algebra but distinct), category theory (morphisms between relations for analogy), dynamical systems theory (Koopman operators, edge of chaos), and lattice theory (CVP, E8 packing). Are these subsumable under the existing five pillars, or do they represent a sixth or seventh pillar that hasn't been recognized? If the synthesis is incomplete, there may be DKC results or eigenflower connections being missed.
