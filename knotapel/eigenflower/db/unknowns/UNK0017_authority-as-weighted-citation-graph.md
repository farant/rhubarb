The transparency document describes argument from authority as having "specific weight depending on the source. Scripture is highest, then Church Fathers, then philosophers. This is essentially a weighted citation graph." But the implementation is unspecified. How are authority weights assigned? Are they fixed (Scripture always highest) or context-dependent (Aristotle carries more weight in natural philosophy than in theology)? How do authority arguments interact with syllogistic arguments — does a strong authority override a weak syllogism? Does the system track chains of authority (Aquinas cites Augustine who cites Paul who cites Christ)? The Summa uses argument from authority extensively ("as the Philosopher says..."), so this mode cannot be ignored.
