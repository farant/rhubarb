The eigenflower CLAUDE.md notes under existing materials: "TODO: Latin Summa text still needed." The English Summa parts are present, but the Latin original is missing from the corpus. For a project focused on theological Latin, training without the Latin Summa is a significant gap. The Latin text is the primary source — the English is a translation with inevitable interpretation choices. Additionally, the fluency layer needs to handle Latin parsing and generation, which requires Latin training data. Sourcing a clean, complete Latin text of the Summa (from Corpus Thomisticum, for example) is a prerequisite for Phase 2 training.
